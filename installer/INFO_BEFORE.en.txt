Windows 11 x64 is currently supported.

This installation includes:
- The validated Microsoft-signed MagicTrackpad2ForWindows v2.0 driver.
- The Magic Trackpad control panel.
- Diagnostic and troubleshooting tools.

Safety boundaries:
- Other Apple, iPhone, or Apple Mobile Device drivers are not modified.
- A newer driver will not be silently downgraded.
- Setup stops for manual review if multiple matching driver packages are detected.

Uninstall preview:
dev.5.4.1 lets you keep the driver or preview removing it with the application.
This preview build never deletes the driver; the validated safe-removal path will
be connected to the user-facing uninstaller in a later stage.
