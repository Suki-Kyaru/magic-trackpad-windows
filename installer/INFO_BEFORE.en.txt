Supported OS for this release candidate: Windows 11 x64. Windows 10 is not supported by the current pinned upstream driver package.

This installation includes:
- The validated Microsoft-signed MagicTrackpad2ForWindows v2.0 driver.
- The Magic Trackpad control panel.
- Diagnostic and troubleshooting tools.

Safety boundaries:
- Other Apple, iPhone, or Apple Mobile Device drivers are not modified.
- A newer driver will not be silently downgraded.
- Setup stops for manual review if multiple matching driver packages are detected.

Uninstall options:
You may remove only the application and keep the driver (recommended), or remove
the Magic Trackpad driver as well.

Driver removal is fail-closed:
- a connected Magic Trackpad blocks driver removal;
- a paired but disconnected device may be removed safely;
- the exact matching package is exported to a backup before deletion;
- /force is never used;
- post-removal verification must report not-installed.
